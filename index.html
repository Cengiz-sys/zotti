Magnet Climb · Mıknatıslı Tırmanış

Clamp, charge, launch.

Zotti is a little magnetic robot repairing a broken tower. Hold to charge, release to launch, and flip your colour in mid-air to land on the right plate. Twelve levels and an endless tower for iPhone and iPad.
